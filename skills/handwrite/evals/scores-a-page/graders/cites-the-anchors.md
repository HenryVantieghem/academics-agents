anchored to. The measured figures in the skill are: a pure font page 22, handtextai 67,
this engine about 68, a real pen page 100.

three anchors, so the number is interpretable.

give one. Also no credit if it presents the score as precise without noting that scores
move by about 5 between seeds, or without noting that the handtextai figure came from a
screenshot of their site rather than a downloaded PDF.
